## Project Overview
BrightCover Insurance is a fictional company created to demonstrate QA/BA processes.  
It issues auto and home policies and manages customer claims.  

## Roles
- Agent: sells policies to customers
- CSR: assists customers with payments and inquiries
- Underwriter: reviews and approves high-risk policies
- Claims Adjuster: investigates and settles claims
